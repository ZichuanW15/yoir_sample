## Evidence rules and remaining work

- **Shared/iOS/Android Jest:** platform component execution, not device, OS,
  keychain, release-build or external-provider acceptance.
- **PostgreSQL API:** real transactions in a new disposable database. Browser
  fixtures and migration engines use their separately identified SQLite databases.
- **Browser:** fresh Web export, isolated API, no retries. Successful responses
  remain real; intentionally delayed/failed responses are named in the tests.
- **Known defects:** advisory assertions and strict XFAILs retain real outcomes.
  Fixed behavior is promoted only with a corresponding regression assertion.
  Green required CI does not establish zero product defects.
- **Native history:** [D02/D03](test-results-native-20261016.md) and the
  [remaining native report](test-results-native-remaining-20261016.md) recorded
  15 PASS / 8 FAIL / 3 BLOCKED across 26 case/platform executions on 16 October.
  They were not repeated on this baseline. Diagnosis integration and historical
  titles changed, so those observations cannot certify the new native behavior.
- **External acceptance:** supplementary tester confirmations for mail, reset,
  payments, offline recovery and Android permission denial remain separate from
  captured runs. No new physical-device, signed-build, real-inbox or live-provider
  result is claimed by this automated update.

Repeat the affected native diagnosis/save/retry/restart/retake/Explore and booking
history/privacy cases before full device sign-off. Retain the unresolved error,
concurrency, accessibility and provider boundaries in the hand-off; closing a QA
issue does not close its parent criteria or repair a defect.
